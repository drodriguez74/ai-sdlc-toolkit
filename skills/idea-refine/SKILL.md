# Idea Refine

## Overview

Expand interpretations, surface tradeoffs, and converge on a recommended
direction.

## When To Use

When the user says "we could maybe…" or asks for ideas. When multiple
implementation paths exist and selection criteria are unclear.

## Workflow

1. Expand 2–4 distinct interpretations of the request.
2. List key assumptions and risks per interpretation.
3. Compare them across the criteria that matter (effort, risk, value, reversibility).
4. Recommend one direction with rationale.
5. Note what would change the recommendation.

## Evidence And Verification

- The recommendation cites at least one criterion the user values.
- Alternatives are not strawmen.

## Common Rationalizations

- "I should pick fast" — picking fast on a vague idea picks the wrong thing.
- "We don't have time for alternatives" — alternatives reveal the binding constraint.

## Red Flags

- Single-option proposal without alternatives.
- Recommendation without rationale.

## Output Contract

A decision summary: 2–4 options with tradeoffs, one recommendation, the
condition that would flip the choice.
